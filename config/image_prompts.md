# Reusable 2D Animation Image Prompts

## Continuity for storytelling shots

Master Moshai is a warm, slightly amused elder with quiet pride in the town. Preserve the same face, glasses, cream cotton panjabi, veranda, lamp and clay cup across shots. Make three tea states: visible steam at the opening, faint steam midway, no steam at the twist and closing. Before a reveal he lifts the cup; at serious moments his face becomes still. No refill. Supporting characters appear only in clearly labeled historical dramatizations; the veranda remains his solo space.

For Episode 1 dramatizations, preserve the same unnamed young adult mechanic in a grey work shirt, carrying a green toolbox with a curved copper-coloured handle. Arrival: stepping from a train, uncertain where to go. Midpoint: setting the toolbox beneath a tea-shop bench, then beside a sleeping mat. Return: walking directly to that same bench as the same seller nods and offers a waiting cup. Leave clear space for the label “কল্পিত চরিত্র ও সংলাপ”; add this text during compositing. These scenes illustrate a composite story, not a named historical person's biography.

## Pose 1: Wide Establishing Shot (Evening Veranda)
Classic 2D storybook illustration inspired by Satyajit Ray's ink and watercolor art style, wide shot of a cozy evening scene on an old Bengali veranda. A lively 60-year-old Bengali schoolmaster ("Master Moshai") with salt-and-pepper hair, thin round spectacles, and a cream cotton panjabi sits alone in an antique cane armchair, leaning forward enthusiastically while narrating a mysterious story. In front of him, a woven floor mat (madur), a closed notebook, and a small wooden table create a quiet storytelling space. On the table sits a glowing vintage brass lamp, a steaming clay cup of tea, and an unrolled 18th-century map of Bengal. Warm golden lantern light against a deep evening blue sky, clean ink lines, soft watercolor textures, 16:9 aspect ratio.

Expressive, animated face revealing a historical secret. Warm golden lighting, clean ink lines, 16:9 aspect ratio.
