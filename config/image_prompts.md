# Reusable 2D Animation Image Prompts

## Pose 1: Wide Establishing Shot (Evening Veranda + Students)
Classic 2D storybook illustration inspired by Satyajit Ray's ink and watercolor art style, wide shot of a cozy evening scene on an old Bengali veranda. A lively 60-year-old Bengali schoolmaster ("Master Moshai") with salt-and-pepper hair, thin round spectacles, and a cream cotton panjabi sits in an antique cane armchair, leaning forward enthusiastically while telling a mysterious story. Sitting attentively on a woven floor mat (madur) in front of him are two curious Bengali teenage students—a boy and a girl—with closed notebooks beside them. On a small wooden table between them sits a glowing vintage brass lamp, a steaming clay cup of tea, and an unrolled 18th-century map of Bengal. Warm golden lantern light against a deep evening blue sky, clean ink lines, soft watercolor textures, 16:9 aspect ratio.
