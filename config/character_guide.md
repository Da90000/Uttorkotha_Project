# Character & Visual Bible: Master Moshai

- **Name:** মাস্টার মশাই (Master Moshai)
- **Age & Look:** 60 years old, lean build, salt-and-pepper hair, thin round spectacles, wearing a simple cream cotton panjabi.
- **Setting:** Evening veranda with a wooden desk, glowing vintage table lamp, clay teacup, antique Bengal maps, and leather-bound colonial gazetteers.
- **Cast:** Master Moshai is the sole narrator and speaking character.
- **Art Style:** Classic 2D storybook illustration inspired by Satyajit Ray's ink and watercolor style (16:9 aspect ratio).
