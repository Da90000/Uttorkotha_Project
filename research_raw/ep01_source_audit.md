## Remaining editorial work

The earlier script assigned exact Gazetteer page ranges from an aggregate bibliography without inspecting those pages. They must not be described as verified claim-to-page citations. Remaining Vas/Majumdar citations in the script are inherited research leads, requiring comparison with original pages: village/hat description, soil terminology, river course and named ports, neighbourhood occupations, and colonial institutions/jute presses. A modern Railway capability list alone cannot date each shop to the founding period. Do not mark the entire episode publication-cleared on the strength of this limited audit.

The composite mechanic has no verified name, birthplace or personal history. Show the dramatization label from his first appearance. Generic explanations about markets, meals and workshop work are illustrative scenes, not additional archival findings.

The timing table counts spoken text only. It is a pacing estimate, not a performed or recorded read-through. A Bengali narrator still needs to record a timed take; local review of neighbourhood names and pronunciation has not occurred.
